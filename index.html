How to play
  1. Study the stadium photo. Pinch or scroll to zoom in on signs, flags and skylines.
  2. Tap the globe to drop your pin where you think it is, then hit Guess.
  3. Closer guesses score more, up to 20 points a round and 100 per game.
  4. Rounds get harder as you go, from famous grounds to hidden gems.
  5. Everyone gets the same Daily Challenge. Share your score when you're done.
